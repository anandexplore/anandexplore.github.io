Background

My work moved from understanding customers, to building the data platforms underneath them, to forecasting and AI that help people decide what to move and when.

I started on Walmart's assortment optimization team, where I built customer and data platforms on Hadoop and Spark. I then moved into customer analytics and cloud data engineering as a senior data scientist, and since 2022 I have worked on supply chain machine learning at Sam's Club and Walmart Global Tech, most recently as a staff machine learning engineer. Outside work I speak at universities and conferences and judge student competitions in Ohio.
